## How "self-manage" was decided
Same test as 157 Leonard: for the owner's whole HPD-registered portfolio, is the registered
managing agent the owner himself / his own office (self), or an outside company (third-party)?
Two corrections learned on this run:
- **DOB "owner contact" is often an expediter** (Gagandeep Singh on 7 entities, Jerome Zwick,
  the Schwimmers). Where that happened the test was re-run on the **title entity's office address**.
- **Mail-drops** (670 Myrtle, 199 Lee, 183 Wilson, 100A Broadway) pool hundreds of unrelated LLCs;
  the office test is void there and the person test was used instead.

"(thin)" = 1–2 prior registrations. "NO HPD HISTORY" = the entity/principal has never registered a
building — first-timer or new-to-NYC operator, no incumbent manager.

## Caveats
- "NO HPD HISTORY" means no registered building under that entity **or** that person's name — a first-timer, or an experienced operator using a fresh name. Half of the A-tier is this category; expect a few to turn out to be known self-managers once you have a real name. Qualify on the first call.
- Unit counts are DOB proposed DUs where a filing exists, else KT's number.
- Office-address tests can be wrong when an owner shares a real office with an unrelated firm; person tests can be wrong when the DOB contact is a rep. Where they disagreed I said so in the CSV `note` column.
